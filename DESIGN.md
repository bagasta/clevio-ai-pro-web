# Clevio AI Pro

Direction: user-supplied monochrome landing-page reference, 30 October 2026. Replaces the earlier navy/lime direction for this page.

Design read: ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 4 / MOTION 2. Bold editorial headlines, near-white paper, one charcoal chapter, thin dividing rules and grayscale photography. The charcoal challenge chapter is the deliberate contrast accent. No decorative geometric hero artwork.

- Seven reading chapters give each topic a screen of its own. Native scroll snapping aligns starts below the sticky header, without intercepting wheel/touch events. Minimum heights keep long chapters readable on phones and short desktop windows.
- Content is visible without entrance animations or timers. The user controls when to move; the page settles at section boundaries. Reduced motion disables snapping and smooth scrolling.
- Manrope supplies the headline voice of the reference. Source Sans 3 keeps long copy legible. Spacing, thin rules and large text provide hierarchy rather than decorative cards or badges.
- Existing local stock photos are marked as temporary illustrations. The sourced portrait identifies the real mentor. No fictitious client logos, testimonials or trust claims.
- Registration and profile retain existing destinations. At the user's request, footer phone and email are now clearly labelled, non-interactive placeholders: `+62 8XX-XXXX-XXXX` and `hello@example.com`. Pre-registration never implies a confirmed seat.
- Footer separates brand, navigation, contact and legal details. It belongs to the closing chapter with the final call to action.
- Responsive floor: 320px. Mobile navigation is a keyboard-accessible disclosure. Photos reflow and chapters grow naturally without fixed-height text cropping.
- Phone reading, up to 760px: one core message per chapter, short benefit labels, and optional native disclosures for supporting copy. The three curriculum stages expand individually. Repeated photo captions and footer navigation are removed from the phone composition. Both ID and EN include concise phone-specific copy; desktop copy remains in place.
- Disclosures start open in HTML for an accessible no-JavaScript fallback. Script closes them at the phone breakpoint and reopens them for desktop. The original minimum-height chapters and reduced-motion support remain.
- Desktop visual narrative: three original AI-generated editorial photographs connect human judgement (hero), the transition in ways of working (challenge), and the material development of an idea into a prototype (curriculum). Shared monochrome tonal range, natural window light, tactile paper, and quiet working gestures connect the series. They are explicitly labelled as AI illustrations, not actual Clevio participants.
- Outcome hierarchy, corrected by the user: the five AI Pro values are HOTS, Design Thinking, AI literacy, leadership and adaptability. These are the primary content, not supporting labels for newly invented benefits. Large numbered pillars use five columns on wide desktops, three on intermediate screens, and five concise rows on phones. All five names and their original short descriptions remain visible outside optional details in ID/EN. Audience and programme format are secondary information.
- Hero copy and image share a two-column desktop composition; challenge and curriculum images support their section headings. On screens up to 960px, images become compact full-width crops: hero 170-220px, challenge 140-180px, curriculum 150-190px. In the mobile hero the image sits between the introductory copy and registration action; curriculum imagery precedes the instruction and expandable stages. Hero is high-priority, supporting imagery is lazy-loaded, and the same lightweight WebP assets are reused. Prompt provenance is in `images/EDITORIAL_ASSETS.md`.
